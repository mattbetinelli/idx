# IdeaXchange User Guide

Welcome to IdeaXchange, the platform where people connect based on shared interests, values, and skills to collaborate on projects!

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

1. Visit the IdeaXchange homepage
2. Click on "Sign Up" in the top right corner
3. Fill in your details (name, email, password)
4. Complete your profile by adding:
   - Profile picture
   - Bio
   - Skills
   - Interests
   - Professional background

### Navigating the Platform

The main navigation menu includes:
- **Dashboard**: Your personalized homepage
- **Ideas**: Browse, create, and collaborate on ideas
- **Virtual Spaces**: Join real-time collaboration spaces
- **Profile**: View and edit your profile
- **Connections**: Manage your network
- **Notifications**: Stay updated on activities

## Core Features

### Ideas

**Creating an Idea**
1. Click "New Idea" on the Ideas page
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - Title
   - Description
   - Problem Statement
   - Proposed Solution
   - Tags (for better discoverability)
3. Click "Submit" to publish your idea

**Interacting with Ideas**
- **Like**: Show appreciation for ideas
- **Comment**: Provide feedback or ask questions
- **Rate**: Evaluate ideas based on innovation, feasibility, market potential, and impact
- **Join Team**: Request to collaborate on an idea

### Virtual Spaces

**Creating a Virtual Space**
1. Click "New Space" on the Virtual Spaces page
2. Set up your space:
   - Title
   - Description
   - Schedule (date/time)
   - Tags
   - Access settings (public/private)
3. Click "Create Space" to launch

**Participating in a Space**
- Join discussions via text chat
- Share resources and links
- Collaborate on documents in real-time
- Schedule follow-up meetings

### Connections

**Finding Connections**
- Browse recommended connections on your Dashboard
- Search for users by name, skills, or interests
- Discover potential collaborators through idea interactions

**Managing Connections**
- Send connection requests
- Accept or decline incoming requests
- Message your connections directly
- Endorse connections for their skills

## Gamification Elements

### XP and Levels

Earn experience points (XP) through platform activities:
- Creating ideas
- Commenting on ideas
- Joining virtual spaces
- Receiving endorsements
- Completing your profile

As you accumulate XP, you'll progress through levels, unlocking new features and recognition.

### Achievements and Badges

**Achievements** are awarded for specific accomplishments:
- First Idea: Create your first idea
- Collaborator: Join 5 virtual spaces
- Networker: Make 10 connections

**Badges** showcase your expertise in specific areas:
- Skill badges (JavaScript Pro, Design Expert)
- Contribution badges (Idea Creator, Helpful Reviewer)
- Community badges (Team Player, Mentor)

### Skill Progression

- Add skills to your profile
- Gain experience in skills through related activities
- Receive endorsements from connections
- Level up skills to showcase your growing expertise

## Tips for Success

1. **Complete your profile** thoroughly to improve matchmaking
2. **Engage regularly** with ideas and virtual spaces
3. **Provide thoughtful feedback** on others' ideas
4. **Build meaningful connections** based on shared interests
5. **Track your progress** through achievements and skill development
